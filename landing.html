How it works

Four steps to meeting survival

01 🔗

Create a session

Hit "Play now" to generate a unique 6-character code and AI-themed session name.

02 📨

Invite your team

Share the code or a one-click join link. Everyone gets a unique randomised board.

03 👂

Tap as you hear them

When a buzzword drops in the meeting, tap it. It marks on every board that has it — instantly.

04 🏆

First to five wins

Land five in a row — horizontal, vertical, or diagonal — and claim bragging rights.
